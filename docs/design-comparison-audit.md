# Comparative design audit — 9 October 2026

Preview reviewed: https://dovindustries-oj96owo9z-dovindustries.vercel.app/art-direction

## Verdict

The page is polished and readable, with a distinctive lead demonstration. It is not yet exceptional as a complete studio website. The previous verdict overstated the result.

The largest gap is how convincingly the page presents the work. Later sections have less substance and specificity than the opening. Additional decorative artwork or spacing changes alone will not resolve that gap.

## Section and transition review

| Part | Current judgment | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header and studio introduction | Clear navigation and recognizable bear identity. The introductory sentence lists activities but gives little sense of the studio's perspective. | Keep the compact header. Develop a factual studio introduction only when there is something specific to say; avoid an invented mission slogan. |

## What the references do better

- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/): featured media connects to a named, linked story. The visitor can continue beyond the presentation.
- [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/): featured releases and smaller listings share clear titles, descriptions, metadata, and destinations. Visual emphasis reflects editorial importance.
- [Work Louder](https://worklouder.cc/): scenes vary considerably in color and scale, while recurring product identifiers and actions hold the page together. Specific hardware remains the subject.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/): feature sections pair a purpose with a product interaction, supporting features, and a path to deeper detail. The sequence follows the work from intake through planning and delivery.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/en-ca): product cards expand into explanations and focused product illustrations. This is especially relevant to DigiDov. Sales overlays add distraction and are not a pattern to copy.

These are useful references for particular strengths, not proof that every part of their sites is superior. Our page should retain its own artwork and voice.

Teenage Engineering's page content was retrieved, but its integrated-browser navigation failed with a connection reset. It is excluded from the visual comparison.

## Broader visual benchmark

A follow-up integrated-browser pass looked beyond software company homepages. This corrects the earlier audit's excessive emphasis on clarity and product evidence: Dovindustries also needs a stronger visual system.

| Reference | Observed visual strength | Relevance to Dovindustries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/) | A large, dense wordmark, contrasting serif introduction, compact navigation, a moving reel, and colorful work tiles create distinct typographic roles. | The website itself has character before individual projects supply their imagery. Our surrounding typography remains conventional. |
| [Isomorphic Labs](https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/) | Outlined modular cells recur in the logo, navigation, and main composition. Light typography, pale color, and biological imagery establish a research identity. | A research company can have a specific visual language without relying on software-marketing conventions. This is a particularly relevant benchmark. |
| [Lusion](https://lusion.co/) | A moving 3D opening and vivid project scenes share a restrained frame and clear labeling. | Motion and art direction can recur across the portfolio rather than appearing only in the hero. |
| [Unseen Studio](https://unseen.co/) | A pink architectural water scene, contrasting italic and sans-serif type, and a project index staged inside the same visual world. | Atmosphere continues beyond the opening. The entry screen and heavy effects are not automatically appropriate for our page. |
| [Igloo](https://www.igloo.inc/) | An icy scene develops into particle-based forms; lighting, camera movement, and compact technical typography create a cinematic identity. | A reference for deliberate reveals and visual continuity, not a reason to replace our drawings with unrelated 3D scenery. |
| [Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/) | Full-width featured work alternates with editorial groups and smaller projects. Different media and palettes remain coherent through consistent presentation. | A broad portfolio can vary in scale and subject while retaining clear curatorial rules. |

These judgments concern visual direction, not an overall ranking or a measured performance claim. The references received desktop visual passes; they were not subjected to full accessibility, mobile, or performance audits.

Instrument and Isomorphic Labs are the most useful additional references for the overall website. Lusion, Unseen, and Igloo raise the ambition for motion and atmosphere. Dovindustries should develop its own system around the actual subjects of drawing and making, with room for project-specific colors.

## Mobile and verification scope

The deployed preview was inspected in the integrated browser on desktop and at a 390 CSS-pixel mobile width. The mobile document width matched the viewport, with no horizontal overflow. Header, demonstration, released projects, DigiDov, secondary research, and contact were inspected.

Mobile stacking is clean, but it exposes the uneven depth: a distinctive demonstration and painting are followed by a long feature panel and a large research question.

## Priorities

1. Make Simply Sefer and DigiDov tangible through one authentic product example each.
2. Give secondary research a consistent, finished structure proportional to the real material available.
3. Improve the paths beyond each presentation, especially Vibe Draw, while preserving the completed bear component.

The test for the next iteration is whether each section leaves the visitor with a specific understanding of the work and a useful next step. Attractive spacing and different colors are already present; the remaining work is substantive editorial and product presentation.
