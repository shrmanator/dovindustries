## DigiDov prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape 4:3 editorial artwork for DigiDov, crypto donations and donor receipts for nonprofits. Input image is ONLY a medium and palette reference, not an edit target; do not include a bear. Create an intimate painterly desk study showing a hand placing one modest old-gold token engraved with the recognizable geometric Ethereum diamond onto a simple ivory donation receipt lying beside an open navy clothbound accounting ledger. The token rests against the paper rather than floating. The receipt has a few restrained charcoal written marks and a small drawn tick, no legible text, no numeric amounts. The ledger's ink lines and worn corners feel used, not ornamental. A single hand enters the frame from the upper right, all important objects fully visible within a generous paper border. Visually express a contribution being recorded, rather than crypto investment. Match the reference's charcoal sketch lines, palette-knife cobalt and slate paint, dry brushwork, textured warm-gray/off-white paper, small muted ochre accents. Serious and tactile editorial fine art, not cartoon or photoreal/3D. Balanced wide 4:3 composition, subject in middle 70%, clear at mobile thumbnail size. No screens, UI, logos, floating coins, arrows, charts, neon, shiny metallic renders, money bags or gratuitous objects.
